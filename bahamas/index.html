    Bahamas population (2026)

    The population of Bahamas is 404,628 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bahamas ranks #178 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.38% a year (+1,596 people). The median age is 36.7 years, women have 1.36 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.0 years. Population density is 40 people per km² of land. The capital is Nassau.
