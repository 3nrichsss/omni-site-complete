    South Sudan population (2026)

    The population of South Sudan is 12,436,037 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. South Sudan ranks #80 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.150% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.02% a year (+247,249 people). The median age is 20.0 years, women have 3.63 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 58.0 years. Population density is 20 people per km² of land. The capital is Juba.
