    Belarus population (2026)

    The population of Belarus is 8,937,018 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Belarus ranks #102 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.108% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.69% a year (-60,585 people). The median age is 42.8 years, women have 1.23 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.0 years. Population density is 44 people per km² of land. The capital is Minsk.
