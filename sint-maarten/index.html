    Sint Maarten population (2026)

    The population of Sint Maarten is 44,447 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Sint Maarten ranks #214 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.11% a year (+524 people). The median age is 43.0 years, women have 1.42 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.9 years. Population density is 1,307 people per km² of land. The capital is Philipsburg.
