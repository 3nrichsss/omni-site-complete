    Niue population (2026)

    The population of Niue is 1,822 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Niue ranks #236 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.33% a year (+2 people). The median age is 36.1 years, women have 2.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 70.4 years. Population density is 7 people per km² of land. The capital is Alofi.
