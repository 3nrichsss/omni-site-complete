    Costa Rica population (2026)

    The population of Costa Rica is 5,174,789 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Costa Rica ranks #127 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.062% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.41% a year (+21,839 people). The median age is 36.7 years, women have 1.31 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.4 years. Population density is 102 people per km² of land. The capital is San José.
