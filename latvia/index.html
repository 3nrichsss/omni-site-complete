    Latvia population (2026)

    The population of Latvia is 1,835,934 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Latvia ranks #151 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.022% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.78% a year (-17,624 people). The median age is 44.8 years, women have 1.35 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.6 years. Population density is 30 people per km² of land. The capital is Riga.
