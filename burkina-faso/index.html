    Burkina Faso population (2026)

    The population of Burkina Faso is 24,601,700 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Burkina Faso ranks #59 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.296% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.15% a year (+527,120 people). The median age is 19.0 years, women have 3.91 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 61.6 years. Population density is 90 people per km² of land. The capital is Ouagadougou.
