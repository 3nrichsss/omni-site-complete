    Greece population (2026)

    The population of Greece is 9,897,116 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Greece ranks #96 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.119% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.43% a year (-41,729 people). The median age is 48.1 years, women have 1.34 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.4 years. Population density is 77 people per km² of land. The capital is Athens.
