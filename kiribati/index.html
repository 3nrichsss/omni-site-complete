    Kiribati population (2026)

    The population of Kiribati is 138,445 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kiribati ranks #193 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.41% a year (+1,957 people). The median age is 24.0 years, women have 3.06 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.9 years. Population density is 171 people per km² of land. The capital is Tarawa.
