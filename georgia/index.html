    Georgia population (2026)

    The population of Georgia is 3,804,642 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Georgia ranks #131 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.046% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.06% a year (-2,029 people). The median age is 38.6 years, women have 1.78 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.0 years. Population density is 55 people per km² of land. The capital is Tbilisi.
