    Guernsey population (2026)

    The population of Guernsey is 64,610 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guernsey ranks #207 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.15% a year (+133 people). The median age is 45.5 years, women have 1.37 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.7 years. Population density is 828 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint Peter Port.
