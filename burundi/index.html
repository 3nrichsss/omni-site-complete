    Burundi population (2026)

    The population of Burundi is 14,729,157 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Burundi ranks #78 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.177% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.31% a year (+339,154 people). The median age is 17.6 years, women have 4.57 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.1 years. Population density is 574 people per km² of land. The capital is Gitega.
