    Mali population (2026)

    The population of Mali is 25,932,275 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mali ranks #58 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.312% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.85% a year (+733,454 people). The median age is 16.9 years, women have 5.33 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 61.1 years. Population density is 21 people per km² of land. The capital is Bamako.
