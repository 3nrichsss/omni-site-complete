    Liberia population (2026)

    The population of Liberia is 5,853,949 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Liberia ranks #117 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.071% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.18% a year (+122,743 people). The median age is 20.0 years, women have 3.71 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 62.6 years. Population density is 61 people per km² of land. The capital is Monrovia.
