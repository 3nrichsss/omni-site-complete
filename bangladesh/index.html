    Bangladesh population (2026)

    The population of Bangladesh is 177,818,044 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bangladesh ranks #8 of 236 countries and territories and holds 2.14% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.19% a year (+2,131,145 people). The median age is 27.3 years, women have 2.09 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.5 years. Population density is 1,366 people per km² of land. The capital is Dhaka.
