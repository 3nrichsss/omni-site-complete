    Gambia population (2026)

    The population of Gambia is 2,884,080 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Gambia ranks #141 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.035% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.15% a year (+61,986 people). The median age is 19.8 years, women have 3.7 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.5 years. Population density is 285 people per km² of land. The capital is Banjul.
