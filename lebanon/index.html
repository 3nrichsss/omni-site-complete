    Lebanon population (2026)

    The population of Lebanon is 5,897,466 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Lebanon ranks #116 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.071% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.84% a year (+48,046 people). The median age is 30.0 years, women have 2.19 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.2 years. Population density is 576 people per km² of land. The capital is Beirut.
