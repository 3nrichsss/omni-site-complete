    Mongolia population (2026)

    The population of Mongolia is 3,556,798 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mongolia ranks #133 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.043% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.09% a year (+39,698 people). The median age is 28.0 years, women have 2.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.5 years. Population density is 2 people per km² of land. The capital is Ulan Bator.
