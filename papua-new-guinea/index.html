    Papua New Guinea population (2026)

    The population of Papua New Guinea is 10,947,848 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Papua New Guinea ranks #89 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.132% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.68% a year (+185,031 people). The median age is 24.0 years, women have 2.99 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.5 years. Population density is 24 people per km² of land. The capital is Port Moresby.
