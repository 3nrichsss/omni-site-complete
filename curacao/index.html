    Curaçao population (2026)

    The population of Curaçao is 185,440 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Curaçao ranks #190 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.03% a year (-46 people). The median age is 40.0 years, women have 1.07 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.3 years. Population density is 418 people per km² of land. The capital is Willemstad.
