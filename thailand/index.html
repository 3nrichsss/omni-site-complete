    Thailand population (2026)

    The population of Thailand is 71,559,614 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Thailand ranks #21 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.862% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.09% a year (-60,249 people). The median age is 42.0 years, women have 1.19 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.1 years. Population density is 140 people per km² of land. The capital is Bangkok.
