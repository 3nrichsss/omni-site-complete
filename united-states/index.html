    United States population (2026)

    The population of United States is 349,035,494 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. United States ranks #3 of 236 countries and territories and holds 4.21% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.49% a year (+1,759,687 people). The median age is 39.7 years, women have 1.62 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.8 years. Population density is 38 people per km² of land. The capital is Washington, D.C..
