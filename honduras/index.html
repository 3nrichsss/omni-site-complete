    Honduras population (2026)

    The population of Honduras is 11,184,760 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Honduras ranks #87 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.135% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.59% a year (+178,910 people). The median age is 25.6 years, women have 2.42 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.4 years. Population density is 100 people per km² of land. The capital is Tegucigalpa.
