    Gabon population (2026)

    The population of Gabon is 2,647,400 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Gabon ranks #145 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.032% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.05% a year (+54,270 people). The median age is 22.6 years, women have 3.49 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.9 years. Population density is 10 people per km² of land. The capital is Libreville.
