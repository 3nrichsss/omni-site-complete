    Saint Martin population (2026)

    The population of Saint Martin is 23,898 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Martin ranks #223 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 4.16% a year (-1,043 people). The median age is 44.9 years, women have 2.59 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.8 years. Population density is 451 people per km² of land. The capital is Marigot.
