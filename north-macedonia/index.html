    North Macedonia population (2026)

    The population of North Macedonia is 1,804,062 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. North Macedonia ranks #152 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.022% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.56% a year (-9,728 people). The median age is 42.4 years, women have 1.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.8 years. Population density is 72 people per km² of land. The capital is Skopje.
