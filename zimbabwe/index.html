    Zimbabwe population (2026)

    The population of Zimbabwe is 17,273,580 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Zimbabwe ranks #74 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.208% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.88% a year (+322,785 people). The median age is 19.3 years, women have 3.57 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 63.6 years. Population density is 45 people per km² of land. The capital is Harare.
