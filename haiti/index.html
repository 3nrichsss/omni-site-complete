    Haiti population (2026)

    The population of Haiti is 12,037,506 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Haiti ranks #82 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.145% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.08% a year (+131,410 people). The median age is 25.3 years, women have 2.55 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 65.5 years. Population density is 437 people per km² of land. The capital is Port-au-Prince.
