    Turkmenistan population (2026)

    The population of Turkmenistan is 7,736,632 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Turkmenistan ranks #104 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.093% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.48% a year (+117,784 people). The median age is 28.3 years, women have 2.6 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 70.5 years. Population density is 16 people per km² of land. The capital is Ashgabat.
