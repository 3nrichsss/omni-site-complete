    Nepal population (2026)

    The population of Nepal is 29,629,410 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Nepal ranks #52 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.357% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.14% a year (+11,292 people). The median age is 26.7 years, women have 1.92 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.2 years. Population density is 207 people per km² of land. The capital is Kathmandu.
