    Tuvalu population (2026)

    The population of Tuvalu is 9,362 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tuvalu ranks #230 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 1.24% a year (-130 people). The median age is 25.1 years, women have 3.1 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.6 years. Population density is 360 people per km² of land. The capital is Funafuti.
