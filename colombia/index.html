    Colombia population (2026)

    The population of Colombia is 53,936,226 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Colombia ranks #28 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.650% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.92% a year (+510,591 people). The median age is 34.0 years, women have 1.6 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.3 years. Population density is 49 people per km² of land. The capital is Bogotá.
