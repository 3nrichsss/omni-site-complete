    Ethiopia population (2026)

    The population of Ethiopia is 138,902,185 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Ethiopia ranks #10 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.67% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.48% a year (+3,430,134 people). The median age is 20.3 years, women have 3.72 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.1 years. Population density is 124 people per km² of land. The capital is Addis Ababa.
