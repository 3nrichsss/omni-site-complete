    Maldives population (2026)

    The population of Maldives is 531,517 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Maldives ranks #174 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.35% a year (+1,841 people). The median age is 34.4 years, women have 1.53 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.7 years. Population density is 1,784 people per km² of land. The capital is Male.
