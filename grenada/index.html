    Grenada population (2026)

    The population of Grenada is 117,362 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Grenada ranks #195 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.03% a year (+59 people). The median age is 35.9 years, women have 1.45 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.7 years. Population density is 341 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint George's.
