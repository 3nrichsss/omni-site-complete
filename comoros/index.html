    Comoros population (2026)

    The population of Comoros is 899,010 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Comoros ranks #163 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.011% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.79% a year (+16,163 people). The median age is 21.7 years, women have 3.71 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.5 years. Population density is 481 people per km² of land. The capital is Moroni.
