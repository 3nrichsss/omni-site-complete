    Benin population (2026)

    The population of Benin is 15,170,420 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Benin ranks #76 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.183% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.36% a year (+355,960 people). The median age is 19.2 years, women have 4.34 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 61.3 years. Population density is 135 people per km² of land. The capital is Porto-Novo.
