    Central African Republic population (2026)

    The population of Central African Republic is 5,698,984 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Central African Republic ranks #118 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.069% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.28% a year (+185,702 people). The median age is 15.6 years, women have 5.69 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 58.2 years. Population density is 9 people per km² of land. The capital is Bangui.
