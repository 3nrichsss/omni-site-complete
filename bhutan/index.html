    Bhutan population (2026)

    The population of Bhutan is 802,214 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bhutan ranks #167 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.010% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.70% a year (+5,533 people). The median age is 32.0 years, women have 1.43 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.8 years. Population density is 21 people per km² of land. The capital is Thimphu.
