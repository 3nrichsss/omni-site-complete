    Lithuania population (2026)

    The population of Lithuania is 2,797,338 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Lithuania ranks #143 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.034% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.82% a year (-32,806 people). The median age is 43.6 years, women have 1.22 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.5 years. Population density is 45 people per km² of land. The capital is Vilnius.
