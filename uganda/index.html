    Uganda population (2026)

    The population of Uganda is 52,761,470 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Uganda ranks #30 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.636% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.62% a year (+1,376,575 people). The median age is 18.1 years, women have 3.97 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.9 years. Population density is 263 people per km² of land. The capital is Kampala.
