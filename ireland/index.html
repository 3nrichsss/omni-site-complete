    Ireland population (2026)

    The population of Ireland is 5,356,950 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Ireland ranks #125 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.065% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.88% a year (+48,911 people). The median age is 40.2 years, women have 1.6 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.9 years. Population density is 78 people per km² of land. The capital is Dublin.
