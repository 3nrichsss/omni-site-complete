    Trinidad and Tobago population (2026)

    The population of Trinidad and Tobago is 1,513,268 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Trinidad and Tobago ranks #155 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.018% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.10% a year (+2,113 people). The median age is 39.2 years, women have 1.52 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.9 years. Population density is 295 people per km² of land. The capital is Port of Spain.
