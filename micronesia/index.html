    Micronesia population (2026)

    The population of Micronesia is 114,182 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Micronesia ranks #196 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.43% a year (+500 people). The median age is 24.5 years, women have 2.67 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.7 years. Population density is 163 people per km² of land. The capital is Palikir - National Government Center.
