    Albania population (2026)

    The population of Albania is 2,751,025 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Albania ranks #144 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.033% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.74% a year (-20,483 people). The median age is 38.8 years, women have 1.32 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.1 years. Population density is 100 people per km² of land. The capital is Tirana.
