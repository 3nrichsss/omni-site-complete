    North Korea population (2026)

    The population of North Korea is 26,633,691 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. North Korea ranks #56 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.321% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.22% a year (+62,655 people). The median age is 37.8 years, women have 1.75 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.0 years. Population density is 221 people per km² of land. The capital is Pyongyang.
