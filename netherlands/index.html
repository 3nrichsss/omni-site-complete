    Netherlands population (2026)

    The population of Netherlands is 18,448,776 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Netherlands ranks #71 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.222% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.51% a year (+101,957 people). The median age is 42.5 years, women have 1.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.6 years. Population density is 548 people per km² of land. The capital is Amsterdam.
