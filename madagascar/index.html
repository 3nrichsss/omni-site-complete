    Madagascar population (2026)

    The population of Madagascar is 33,522,052 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Madagascar ranks #49 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.404% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.34% a year (+781,374 people). The median age is 20.4 years, women have 3.78 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.3 years. Population density is 58 people per km² of land. The capital is Antananarivo.
