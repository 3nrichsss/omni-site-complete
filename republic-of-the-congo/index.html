    Republic of the Congo population (2026)

    The population of Republic of the Congo is 6,637,786 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Republic of the Congo ranks #112 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.080% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.33% a year (+153,349 people). The median age is 19.7 years, women have 3.99 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.4 years. Population density is 19 people per km² of land. The capital is Brazzaville.
