    Liechtenstein population (2026)

    The population of Liechtenstein is 40,368 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Liechtenstein ranks #217 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.57% a year (+240 people). The median age is 45.7 years, women have 1.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.1 years. Population density is 252 people per km² of land. The capital is Vaduz.
