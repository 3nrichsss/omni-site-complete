    Pakistan population (2026)

    The population of Pakistan is 259,299,792 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Pakistan ranks #5 of 236 countries and territories and holds 3.12% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.59% a year (+4,080,237 people). The median age is 21.8 years, women have 3.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.1 years. Population density is 336 people per km² of land. The capital is Islamabad.
