    Sierra Leone population (2026)

    The population of Sierra Leone is 8,996,744 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Sierra Leone ranks #101 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.108% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.96% a year (+176,951 people). The median age is 20.9 years, women have 3.53 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 62.3 years. Population density is 125 people per km² of land. The capital is Freetown.
