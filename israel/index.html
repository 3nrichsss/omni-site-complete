    Israel population (2026)

    The population of Israel is 9,647,689 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Israel ranks #97 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.116% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.36% a year (+130,508 people). The median age is 30.3 years, women have 2.73 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.9 years. Population density is 446 people per km² of land. The capital is Jerusalem.
