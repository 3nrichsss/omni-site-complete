    Czechia population (2026)

    The population of Czechia is 10,527,780 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Czechia ranks #92 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.127% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.20% a year (-81,459 people). The median age is 45.3 years, women have 1.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.3 years. Population density is 136 people per km² of land. The capital is Prague.
