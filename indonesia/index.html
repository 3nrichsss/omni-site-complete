    Indonesia population (2026)

    The population of Indonesia is 287,886,782 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Indonesia ranks #4 of 236 countries and territories and holds 3.47% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.74% a year (+2,165,547 people). The median age is 31.7 years, women have 2.08 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.6 years. Population density is 152 people per km² of land. The capital is Jakarta.
