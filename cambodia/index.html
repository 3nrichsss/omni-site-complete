    Cambodia population (2026)

    The population of Cambodia is 18,051,220 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cambodia ranks #73 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.217% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.10% a year (+203,238 people). The median age is 27.4 years, women have 2.49 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.1 years. Population density is 102 people per km² of land. The capital is Phnom Penh.
