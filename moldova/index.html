    Moldova population (2026)

    The population of Moldova is 2,961,254 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Moldova ranks #139 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.036% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.98% a year (-34,852 people). The median age is 40.1 years, women have 1.72 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.6 years. Population density is 90 people per km² of land. The capital is Chisinau.
