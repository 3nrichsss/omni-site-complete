    Hong Kong population (2026)

    The population of Hong Kong is 7,378,602 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Hong Kong ranks #107 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.089% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.46% a year (-17,474 people). The median age is 48.9 years, women have 0.75 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 85.9 years. Population density is 7,027 people per km² of land. The capital is Hong Kong.
