    Suriname population (2026)

    The population of Suriname is 645,256 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Suriname ranks #170 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.83% a year (+5,406 people). The median age is 29.9 years, women have 2.19 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.0 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Paramaribo.
