    Germany population (2026)

    The population of Germany is 83,644,258 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Germany ranks #19 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.23% a year (-430,817 people). The median age is 46.7 years, women have 1.46 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.9 years. Population density is 239 people per km² of land. The capital is Berlin.
