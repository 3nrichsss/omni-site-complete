    Caribbean Netherlands population (2026)

    The population of Caribbean Netherlands is 31,913 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Caribbean Netherlands ranks #222 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.65% a year (+575 people). The median age is 40.7 years, women have 1.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.0 years. Population density is 97 people per km² of land. The capital is Kralendijk.
