    Norway population (2026)

    The population of Norway is 5,652,988 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Norway ranks #121 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.068% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.37% a year (+29,918 people). The median age is 41.1 years, women have 1.42 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.8 years. Population density is 16 people per km² of land. The capital is Oslo.
