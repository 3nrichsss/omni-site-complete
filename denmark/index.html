    Denmark population (2026)

    The population of Denmark is 6,023,520 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Denmark ranks #114 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.073% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.31% a year (+21,014 people). The median age is 42.3 years, women have 1.52 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.4 years. Population density is 151 people per km² of land. The capital is Copenhagen.
