    Philippines population (2026)

    The population of Philippines is 117,724,472 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Philippines ranks #14 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.42% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.79% a year (+937,509 people). The median age is 27.6 years, women have 1.86 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 70.2 years. Population density is 395 people per km² of land. The capital is Manila.
