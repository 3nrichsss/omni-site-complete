    Guyana population (2026)

    The population of Guyana is 840,890 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guyana ranks #166 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.010% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.58% a year (+4,904 people). The median age is 27.5 years, women have 2.35 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 70.6 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Georgetown.
