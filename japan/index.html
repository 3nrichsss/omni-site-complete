    Japan population (2026)

    The population of Japan is 122,427,731 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Japan ranks #12 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.48% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.56% a year (-675,748 people). The median age is 51.2 years, women have 1.23 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 85.1 years. Population density is 336 people per km² of land. The capital is Tokyo.
