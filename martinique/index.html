    Martinique population (2026)

    The population of Martinique is 337,711 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Martinique ranks #183 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.80% a year (-2,728 people). The median age is 51.2 years, women have 1.95 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.0 years. Population density is 299 people per km² of land. The capital is Fort-de-France.
