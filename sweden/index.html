    Sweden population (2026)

    The population of Sweden is 10,701,046 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Sweden ranks #91 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.129% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.39% a year (+44,414 people). The median age is 41.5 years, women have 1.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.7 years. Population density is 26 people per km² of land. The capital is Stockholm.
