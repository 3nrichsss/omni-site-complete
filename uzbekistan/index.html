    Uzbekistan population (2026)

    The population of Uzbekistan is 37,724,222 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Uzbekistan ranks #43 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.454% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.75% a year (+670,795 people). The median age is 28.0 years, women have 3.4 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.8 years. Population density is 86 people per km² of land. The capital is Tashkent.
