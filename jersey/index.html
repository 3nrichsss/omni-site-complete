    Jersey population (2026)

    The population of Jersey is 104,106 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Jersey ranks #198 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.10% a year (+117 people). The median age is 44.9 years, women have 1.38 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.1 years. Population density is 897 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint Helier.
