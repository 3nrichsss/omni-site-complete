    Mayotte population (2026)

    The population of Mayotte is 347,536 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mayotte ranks #181 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.04% a year (+10,525 people). The median age is 18.3 years, women have 4.43 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.6 years. Population density is 929 people per km² of land. The capital is Mamoudzou.
