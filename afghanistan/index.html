    Afghanistan population (2026)

    The population of Afghanistan is 45,047,068 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Afghanistan ranks #36 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.543% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.71% a year (+1,202,957 people). The median age is 18.4 years, women have 4.56 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.8 years. Population density is 69 people per km² of land. The capital is Kabul.
