    Timor-Leste population (2026)

    The population of Timor-Leste is 1,436,923 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Timor-Leste ranks #156 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.017% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.31% a year (+18,406 people). The median age is 23.1 years, women have 2.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.3 years. Population density is 97 people per km² of land. The capital is Dili.
