    India population (2026)

    The population of India is 1,476,625,576 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. India ranks #1 of 236 countries and territories and holds 17.8% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.86% a year (+12,760,052 people). The median age is 30.2 years, women have 1.93 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.7 years. Population density is 497 people per km² of land. The capital is New Delhi.
