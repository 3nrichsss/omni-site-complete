    Eswatini population (2026)

    The population of Eswatini is 1,269,858 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Eswatini ranks #159 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.015% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.08% a year (+13,685 people). The median age is 23.7 years, women have 2.64 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.5 years. Population density is 74 people per km² of land. The capital is Mbabane.
