    Tunisia population (2026)

    The population of Tunisia is 12,415,138 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tunisia ranks #81 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.150% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.52% a year (+66,566 people). The median age is 34.4 years, women have 1.79 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.1 years. Population density is 80 people per km² of land. The capital is Tunis.
