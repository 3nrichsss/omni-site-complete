    Azerbaijan population (2026)

    The population of Azerbaijan is 10,454,855 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Azerbaijan ranks #93 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.126% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.53% a year (+57,142 people). The median age is 35.1 years, women have 1.66 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.9 years. Population density is 126 people per km² of land. The capital is Baku.
