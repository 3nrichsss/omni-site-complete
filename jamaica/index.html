    Jamaica population (2026)

    The population of Jamaica is 2,833,404 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Jamaica ranks #142 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.034% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.16% a year (-3,674 people). The median age is 34.3 years, women have 1.33 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.9 years. Population density is 262 people per km² of land. The capital is Kingston.
