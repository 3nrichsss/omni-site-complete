    Switzerland population (2026)

    The population of Switzerland is 9,007,798 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Switzerland ranks #100 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.109% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.42% a year (+40,391 people). The median age is 44.2 years, women have 1.45 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.4 years. Population density is 228 people per km² of land. The capital is Bern.
