    China population (2026)

    The population of China is 1,412,914,088 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. China ranks #2 of 236 countries and territories and holds 17.0% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.22% a year (-3,182,005 people). The median age is 41.7 years, women have 1.03 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.0 years. Population density is 150 people per km² of land. The capital is Beijing.
