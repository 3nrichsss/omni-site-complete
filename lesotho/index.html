    Lesotho population (2026)

    The population of Lesotho is 2,389,336 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Lesotho ranks #147 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.029% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.09% a year (+26,011 people). The median age is 23.1 years, women have 2.61 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 58.6 years. Population density is 79 people per km² of land. The capital is Maseru.
