    Bosnia and Herzegovina population (2026)

    The population of Bosnia and Herzegovina is 3,114,242 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bosnia and Herzegovina ranks #138 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.038% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.84% a year (-25,854 people). The median age is 47.1 years, women have 1.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.4 years. Population density is 61 people per km² of land. The capital is Sarajevo.
