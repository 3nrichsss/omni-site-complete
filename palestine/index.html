    Palestine population (2026)

    The population of Palestine is 5,692,790 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Palestine ranks #119 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.069% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.90% a year (+103,167 people). The median age is 21.3 years, women have 3.13 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.4 years. Population density is 945 people per km² of land. The capital is East Jerusalem.
