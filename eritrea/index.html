    Eritrea population (2026)

    The population of Eritrea is 3,682,669 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Eritrea ranks #132 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.044% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.07% a year (+75,666 people). The median age is 20.5 years, women have 3.55 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.4 years. Population density is 30 people per km² of land. The capital is Asmara.
