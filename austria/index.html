    Austria population (2026)

    The population of Austria is 9,107,266 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Austria ranks #99 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.110% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.07% a year (-6,308 people). The median age is 44.9 years, women have 1.34 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.5 years. Population density is 110 people per km² of land. The capital is Vienna.
