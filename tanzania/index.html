    Tanzania population (2026)

    The population of Tanzania is 72,563,780 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tanzania ranks #20 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.874% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.79% a year (+2,017,915 people). The median age is 18.6 years, women have 4.41 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.6 years. Population density is 82 people per km² of land. The capital is Dodoma.
