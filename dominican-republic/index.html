    Dominican Republic population (2026)

    The population of Dominican Republic is 11,609,500 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Dominican Republic ranks #84 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.140% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.75% a year (+89,013 people). The median age is 29.7 years, women have 2.17 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.1 years. Population density is 241 people per km² of land. The capital is Santo Domingo.
