    Saint Pierre and Miquelon population (2026)

    The population of Saint Pierre and Miquelon is 5,514 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Pierre and Miquelon ranks #231 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 1.14% a year (-60 people). The median age is 48.6 years, women have 1.28 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.3 years. Population density is 23 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint-Pierre.
