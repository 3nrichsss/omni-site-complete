    Venezuela population (2026)

    The population of Venezuela is 28,633,710 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Venezuela ranks #54 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.345% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.42% a year (+116,815 people). The median age is 30.5 years, women have 2.04 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.0 years. Population density is 32 people per km² of land. The capital is Caracas.
