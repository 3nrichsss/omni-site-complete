    Mauritania population (2026)

    The population of Mauritania is 5,461,319 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mauritania ranks #123 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.066% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.69% a year (+146,254 people). The median age is 18.5 years, women have 4.48 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.2 years. Population density is 5 people per km² of land. The capital is Nouakchott.
