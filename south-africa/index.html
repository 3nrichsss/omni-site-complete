    South Africa population (2026)

    The population of South Africa is 65,453,084 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. South Africa ranks #24 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.789% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.05% a year (+705,765 people). The median age is 29.9 years, women have 2.17 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.7 years. Population density is 54 people per km² of land. The capital is Pretoria.
