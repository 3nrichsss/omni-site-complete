    Fiji population (2026)

    The population of Fiji is 937,282 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Fiji ranks #162 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.011% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.43% a year (+4,127 people). The median age is 29.3 years, women have 2.23 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.8 years. Population density is 51 people per km² of land. The capital is Suva.
