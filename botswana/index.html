    Botswana population (2026)

    The population of Botswana is 2,603,388 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Botswana ranks #146 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.031% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.60% a year (+41,266 people). The median age is 24.7 years, women have 2.63 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.6 years. Population density is 5 people per km² of land. The capital is Gaborone.
