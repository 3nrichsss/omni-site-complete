    Croatia population (2026)

    The population of Croatia is 3,822,345 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Croatia ranks #130 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.046% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.66% a year (-25,815 people). The median age is 46.5 years, women have 1.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.1 years. Population density is 68 people per km² of land. The capital is Zagreb.
