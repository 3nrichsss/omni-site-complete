    Sri Lanka population (2026)

    The population of Sri Lanka is 23,348,315 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Sri Lanka ranks #60 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.281% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.50% a year (+118,844 people). The median age is 34.6 years, women have 1.93 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.0 years. Population density is 377 people per km² of land. The capital is Colombo.
