    Niger population (2026)

    The population of Niger is 28,814,878 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Niger ranks #53 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.347% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.13% a year (+897,047 people). The median age is 16.7 years, women have 5.64 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 61.9 years. Population density is 23 people per km² of land. The capital is Niamey.
