    Ghana population (2026)

    The population of Ghana is 35,697,557 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Ghana ranks #46 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.430% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.77% a year (+633,285 people). The median age is 22.6 years, women have 3.26 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.1 years. Population density is 157 people per km² of land. The capital is Accra.
