    Tokelau population (2026)

    The population of Tokelau is 2,691 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tokelau ranks #235 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.60% a year (+83 people). The median age is 28.6 years, women have 2.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.4 years. Population density is 224 people per km² of land. The capital is Atafu Village.
