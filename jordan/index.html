    Jordan population (2026)

    The population of Jordan is 11,589,532 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Jordan ranks #85 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.140% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.12% a year (+68,848 people). The median age is 25.9 years, women have 2.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.3 years. Population density is 131 people per km² of land. The capital is Amman.
