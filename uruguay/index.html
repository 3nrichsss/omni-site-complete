    Uruguay population (2026)

    The population of Uruguay is 3,382,537 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Uruguay ranks #134 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.041% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.07% a year (-2,151 people). The median age is 37.7 years, women have 1.38 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.6 years. Population density is 19 people per km² of land. The capital is Montevideo.
