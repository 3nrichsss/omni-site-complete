    Cameroon population (2026)

    The population of Cameroon is 30,640,816 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cameroon ranks #51 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.369% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.49% a year (+761,480 people). The median age is 19.2 years, women have 4.12 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.5 years. Population density is 65 people per km² of land. The capital is Yaoundé.
