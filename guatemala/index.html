    Guatemala population (2026)

    The population of Guatemala is 18,967,978 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guatemala ranks #69 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.229% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.47% a year (+280,096 people). The median age is 24.8 years, women have 2.23 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.0 years. Population density is 177 people per km² of land. The capital is Guatemala City.
