    French Guiana population (2026)

    The population of French Guiana is 318,871 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. French Guiana ranks #184 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.65% a year (+5,206 people). The median age is 25.9 years, women have 3.25 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.5 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Cayenne.
