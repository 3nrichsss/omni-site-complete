    Myanmar population (2026)

    The population of Myanmar is 55,184,819 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Myanmar ranks #27 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.665% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.59% a year (+334,171 people). The median age is 31.4 years, women have 2.06 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.5 years. Population density is 85 people per km² of land. The capital is Nay Pyi Taw.
