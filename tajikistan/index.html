    Tajikistan population (2026)

    The population of Tajikistan is 10,978,598 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tajikistan ranks #88 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.132% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.74% a year (+191,865 people). The median age is 23.4 years, women have 2.96 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.2 years. Population density is 79 people per km² of land. The capital is Dushanbe.
