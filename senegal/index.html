    Senegal population (2026)

    The population of Senegal is 19,366,548 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Senegal ranks #68 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.233% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.26% a year (+434,582 people). The median age is 20.8 years, women have 3.66 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.4 years. Population density is 101 people per km² of land. The capital is Dakar.
