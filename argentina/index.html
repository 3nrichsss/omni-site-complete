    Argentina population (2026)

    The population of Argentina is 46,003,734 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Argentina ranks #35 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.554% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.33% a year (+152,356 people). The median age is 34.3 years, women have 1.51 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.8 years. Population density is 17 people per km² of land. The capital is Buenos Aires.
