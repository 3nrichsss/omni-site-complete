    Türkiye population (2026)

    The population of Türkiye is 87,926,082 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Türkiye ranks #18 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.06% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.30% a year (+240,656 people). The median age is 34.9 years, women have 1.62 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.4 years. Population density is 114 people per km² of land. The capital is Ankara.
