    Ukraine population (2026)

    The population of Ukraine is 39,535,848 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Ukraine ranks #40 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.476% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.74% a year (+555,472 people). The median age is 42.8 years, women have 1.0 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.0 years. Population density is 68 people per km² of land. The capital is Kyiv.
