    Cuba population (2026)

    The population of Cuba is 10,892,659 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cuba ranks #90 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.131% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.42% a year (-44,544 people). The median age is 43.5 years, women have 1.45 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.6 years. Population density is 105 people per km² of land. The capital is Havana.
