    Syria population (2026)

    The population of Syria is 26,472,497 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Syria ranks #57 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.319% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.04% a year (+852,071 people). The median age is 24.8 years, women have 2.62 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.4 years. Population density is 144 people per km² of land. The capital is Damascus.
