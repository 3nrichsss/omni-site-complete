    Mexico population (2026)

    The population of Mexico is 132,997,658 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mexico ranks #11 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.60% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.78% a year (+1,050,758 people). The median age is 31.0 years, women have 1.85 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.6 years. Population density is 68 people per km² of land. The capital is Mexico City.
