    Tonga population (2026)

    The population of Tonga is 103,291 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Tonga ranks #199 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.43% a year (-451 people). The median age is 22.0 years, women have 3.04 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.4 years. Population density is 143 people per km² of land. The capital is Nuku‘alofa.
