    Kazakhstan population (2026)

    The population of Kazakhstan is 21,083,626 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kazakhstan ranks #65 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.254% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.11% a year (+239,872 people). The median age is 30.7 years, women have 2.92 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.8 years. Population density is 8 people per km² of land. The capital is Nur-Sultan.
