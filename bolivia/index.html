    Bolivia population (2026)

    The population of Bolivia is 12,749,291 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bolivia ranks #79 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.154% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.31% a year (+167,448 people). The median age is 26.5 years, women have 2.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.1 years. Population density is 12 people per km² of land. The capital is Sucre.
