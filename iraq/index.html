    Iraq population (2026)

    The population of Iraq is 48,007,437 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Iraq ranks #33 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.578% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.05% a year (+986,663 people). The median age is 22.1 years, women have 3.13 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.6 years. Population density is 111 people per km² of land. The capital is Baghdad.
