    Kyrgyzstan population (2026)

    The population of Kyrgyzstan is 7,400,464 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kyrgyzstan ranks #106 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.089% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.40% a year (+105,430 people). The median age is 26.5 years, women have 2.72 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.1 years. Population density is 39 people per km² of land. The capital is Bishkek.
