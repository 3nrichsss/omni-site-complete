    Armenia population (2026)

    The population of Armenia is 2,930,915 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Armenia ranks #140 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.035% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.72% a year (-21,450 people). The median age is 38.2 years, women have 1.71 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.2 years. Population density is 104 people per km² of land. The capital is Yerevan.
