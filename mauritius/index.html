    Mauritius population (2026)

    The population of Mauritius is 1,265,058 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mauritius ranks #160 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.015% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.27% a year (-3,221 people). The median age is 39.3 years, women have 1.21 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.4 years. Population density is 633 people per km² of land. The capital is Port Louis.
