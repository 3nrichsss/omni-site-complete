    Saint Kitts and Nevis population (2026)

    The population of Saint Kitts and Nevis is 46,992 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Kitts and Nevis ranks #212 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.13% a year (+70 people). The median age is 37.5 years, women have 1.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.6 years. Population density is 180 people per km² of land. The capital is Basseterre.
