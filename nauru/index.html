    Nauru population (2026)

    The population of Nauru is 12,102 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Nauru ranks #227 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.64% a year (+76 people). The median age is 21.2 years, women have 3.2 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 62.6 years. Population density is 576 people per km² of land. The capital is Yaren.
