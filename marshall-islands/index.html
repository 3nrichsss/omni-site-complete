    Marshall Islands population (2026)

    The population of Marshall Islands is 35,075 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Marshall Islands ranks #220 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 3.28% a year (-1,207 people). The median age is 21.5 years, women have 2.77 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.3 years. Population density is 194 people per km² of land. The capital is Majuro.
