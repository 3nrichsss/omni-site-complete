    Réunion population (2026)

    The population of Réunion is 886,298 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Réunion ranks #164 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.011% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.45% a year (+3,893 people). The median age is 39.4 years, women have 2.12 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.9 years. Population density is 353 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint-Denis.
