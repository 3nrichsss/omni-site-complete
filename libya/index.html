    Libya population (2026)

    The population of Libya is 7,539,852 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Libya ranks #105 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.091% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.12% a year (+81,297 people). The median age is 29.0 years, women have 2.21 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.2 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Tripoli.
