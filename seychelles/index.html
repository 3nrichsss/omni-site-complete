    Seychelles population (2026)

    The population of Seychelles is 134,959 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Seychelles ranks #194 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.53% a year (+2,180 people). The median age is 35.6 years, women have 2.06 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.3 years. Population density is 295 people per km² of land. The capital is Victoria.
