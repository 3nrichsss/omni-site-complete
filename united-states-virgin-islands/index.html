    United States Virgin Islands population (2026)

    The population of United States Virgin Islands is 83,400 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. United States Virgin Islands ranks #204 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.89% a year (-738 people). The median age is 45.9 years, women have 2.05 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.2 years. Population density is 240 people per km² of land. The capital is Charlotte Amalie.
