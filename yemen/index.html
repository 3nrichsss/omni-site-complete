    Yemen population (2026)

    The population of Yemen is 42,961,653 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Yemen ranks #37 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.518% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.76% a year (+1,187,775 people). The median age is 19.5 years, women have 4.33 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.7 years. Population density is 81 people per km² of land. The capital is Sanaa.
