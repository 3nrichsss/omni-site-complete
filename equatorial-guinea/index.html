    Equatorial Guinea population (2026)

    The population of Equatorial Guinea is 1,984,468 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Equatorial Guinea ranks #150 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.024% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.32% a year (+46,037 people). The median age is 21.9 years, women have 3.95 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.3 years. Population density is 71 people per km² of land. The capital is Malabo.
