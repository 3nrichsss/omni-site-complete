    Finland population (2026)

    The population of Finland is 5,621,738 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Finland ranks #122 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.068% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.08% a year (-1,591 people). The median age is 44.4 years, women have 1.3 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.4 years. Population density is 18 people per km² of land. The capital is Helsinki.
