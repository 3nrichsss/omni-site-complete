    Togo population (2026)

    The population of Togo is 9,930,918 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Togo ranks #95 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.120% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.13% a year (+209,311 people). The median age is 20.2 years, women have 4.01 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 63.3 years. Population density is 183 people per km² of land. The capital is Lomé.
