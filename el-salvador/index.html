    El Salvador population (2026)

    The population of El Salvador is 6,391,254 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. El Salvador ranks #113 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.077% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.40% a year (+25,751 people). The median age is 29.4 years, women have 1.74 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.7 years. Population density is 308 people per km² of land. The capital is San Salvador.
