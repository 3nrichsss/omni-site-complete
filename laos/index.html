    Laos population (2026)

    The population of Laos is 7,974,018 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Laos ranks #103 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.096% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.25% a year (+100,972 people). The median age is 26.3 years, women have 2.33 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.7 years. Population density is 35 people per km² of land. The capital is Vientiane.
