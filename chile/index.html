    Chile population (2026)

    The population of Chile is 19,945,850 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Chile ranks #67 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.240% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.41% a year (+85,929 people). The median age is 38.4 years, women have 1.12 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.7 years. Population density is 27 people per km² of land. The capital is Santiago.
