    Russia population (2026)

    The population of Russia is 143,394,458 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Russia ranks #9 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.73% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.23% a year (-602,935 people). The median age is 41.7 years, women have 1.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.7 years. Population density is 9 people per km² of land. The capital is Moscow.
