    Ecuador population (2026)

    The population of Ecuador is 18,444,506 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Ecuador ranks #72 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.222% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.84% a year (+154,610 people). The median age is 30.7 years, women have 1.78 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.9 years. Population density is 74 people per km² of land. The capital is Quito.
