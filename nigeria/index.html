    Nigeria population (2026)

    The population of Nigeria is 242,431,832 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Nigeria ranks #6 of 236 countries and territories and holds 2.92% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.03% a year (+4,904,050 people). The median age is 19.4 years, women have 4.2 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 55.0 years. Population density is 266 people per km² of land. The capital is Abuja.
