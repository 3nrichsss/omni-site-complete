    Turks and Caicos Islands population (2026)

    The population of Turks and Caicos Islands is 47,148 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Turks and Caicos Islands ranks #211 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.58% a year (+294 people). The median age is 40.6 years, women have 1.43 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.5 years. Population density is 50 people per km² of land. The capital is Cockburn Town.
