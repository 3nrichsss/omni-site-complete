    Malta population (2026)

    The population of Malta is 549,011 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Malta ranks #173 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.37% a year (+3,606 people). The median age is 42.5 years, women have 1.12 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.8 years. Population density is 1,742 people per km² of land. The capital is Valletta.
