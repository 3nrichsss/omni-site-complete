    France population (2026)

    The population of France is 66,746,400 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. France ranks #23 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.804% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.14% a year (+95,596 people). The median age is 43.5 years, women have 1.64 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.7 years. Population density is 124 people per km² of land. The capital is Paris.
