    Mozambique population (2026)

    The population of Mozambique is 36,639,850 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Mozambique ranks #44 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.441% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.79% a year (+1,008,198 people). The median age is 17.6 years, women have 4.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.1 years. Population density is 47 people per km² of land. The capital is Maputo.
