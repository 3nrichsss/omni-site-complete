    Australia population (2026)

    The population of Australia is 27,227,096 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Australia ranks #55 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.328% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.91% a year (+253,070 people). The median age is 39.5 years, women have 1.64 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.3 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Canberra.
