    United Arab Emirates population (2026)

    The population of United Arab Emirates is 11,574,682 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. United Arab Emirates ranks #86 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.139% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.69% a year (+228,682 people). The median age is 32.6 years, women have 1.21 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.4 years. Population density is 163 people per km² of land. The capital is Abu Dhabi.
