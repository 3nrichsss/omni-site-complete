    Cyprus population (2026)

    The population of Cyprus is 1,382,334 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cyprus ranks #157 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.017% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.80% a year (+11,581 people). The median age is 40.1 years, women have 1.37 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.2 years. Population density is 150 people per km² of land. The capital is Nicosia.
