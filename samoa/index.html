    Samoa population (2026)

    The population of Samoa is 220,528 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Samoa ranks #189 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.56% a year (+1,221 people). The median age is 20.8 years, women have 3.7 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.1 years. Population density is 79 people per km² of land. The capital is Apia.
