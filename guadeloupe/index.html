    Guadeloupe population (2026)

    The population of Guadeloupe is 372,453 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guadeloupe ranks #180 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.36% a year (-1,338 people). The median age is 48.4 years, women have 2.04 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.6 years. Population density is 229 people per km² of land. The capital is Basse-Terre.
