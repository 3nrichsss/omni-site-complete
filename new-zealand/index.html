    New Zealand population (2026)

    The population of New Zealand is 5,287,479 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. New Zealand ranks #126 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.064% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.65% a year (+35,580 people). The median age is 39.0 years, women have 1.64 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.5 years. Population density is 20 people per km² of land. The capital is Wellington.
