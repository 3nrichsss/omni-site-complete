    Vanuatu population (2026)

    The population of Vanuatu is 342,564 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Vanuatu ranks #182 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.16% a year (+7,395 people). The median age is 21.4 years, women have 3.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.0 years. Population density is 28 people per km² of land. The capital is Port-Vila.
