    Poland population (2026)

    The population of Poland is 37,843,188 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Poland ranks #42 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.456% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.38% a year (-297,721 people). The median age is 44.0 years, women have 1.31 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.2 years. Population density is 124 people per km² of land. The capital is Warsaw.
