    Djibouti population (2026)

    The population of Djibouti is 1,199,460 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Djibouti ranks #161 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.014% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.31% a year (+15,383 people). The median age is 26.2 years, women have 2.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.6 years. Population density is 52 people per km² of land. The capital is Djibouti.
