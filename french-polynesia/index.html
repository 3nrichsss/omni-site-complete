    French Polynesia population (2026)

    The population of French Polynesia is 283,076 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. French Polynesia ranks #186 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.21% a year (+611 people). The median age is 37.7 years, women have 1.47 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.4 years. Population density is 80 people per km² of land. The capital is Papeete.
