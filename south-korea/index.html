    South Korea population (2026)

    The population of South Korea is 51,600,388 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. South Korea ranks #31 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.622% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.14% a year (-66,641 people). The median age is 47.2 years, women have 0.76 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.6 years. Population density is 529 people per km² of land. The capital is Seoul.
