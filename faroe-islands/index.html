    Faroe Islands population (2026)

    The population of Faroe Islands is 56,526 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Faroe Islands ranks #209 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.87% a year (+525 people). The median age is 38.1 years, women have 2.19 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.8 years. Population density is 41 people per km² of land. The capital is Tórshavn.
