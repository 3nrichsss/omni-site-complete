    Romania population (2026)

    The population of Romania is 18,800,604 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Romania ranks #70 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.226% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.58% a year (-108,046 people). The median age is 44.4 years, women have 1.71 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.4 years. Population density is 82 people per km² of land. The capital is Bucharest.
