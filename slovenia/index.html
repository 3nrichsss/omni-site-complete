    Slovenia population (2026)

    The population of Slovenia is 2,114,574 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Slovenia ranks #149 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.025% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.13% a year (-2,499 people). The median age is 46.0 years, women have 1.58 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.1 years. Population density is 105 people per km² of land. The capital is Ljubljana.
