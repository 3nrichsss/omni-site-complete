    British Virgin Islands population (2026)

    The population of British Virgin Islands is 39,936 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. British Virgin Islands ranks #218 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.44% a year (+204 people). The median age is 40.1 years, women have 1.07 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.8 years. Population density is 264 people per km² of land. The capital is Road Town.
