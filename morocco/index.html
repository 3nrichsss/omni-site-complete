    Morocco population (2026)

    The population of Morocco is 38,762,441 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Morocco ranks #41 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.467% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.84% a year (+331,671 people). The median age is 31.1 years, women have 2.16 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.9 years. Population density is 87 people per km² of land. The capital is Rabat.
