    Bermuda population (2026)

    The population of Bermuda is 64,459 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bermuda ranks #208 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.15% a year (-96 people). The median age is 47.4 years, women have 1.42 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.8 years. Population density is 1,194 people per km² of land. The capital is Hamilton.
