    Anguilla population (2026)

    The population of Anguilla is 14,817 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Anguilla ranks #225 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.47% a year (+89 people). The median age is 40.1 years, women have 1.34 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.9 years. Population density is 163 people per km² of land. The capital is The Valley.
