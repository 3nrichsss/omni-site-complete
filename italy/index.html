    Italy population (2026)

    The population of Italy is 58,926,166 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Italy ranks #25 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.710% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.39% a year (-220,094 people). The median age is 49.6 years, women have 1.22 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.2 years. Population density is 199 people per km² of land. The capital is Rome.
