    Sudan population (2026)

    The population of Sudan is 53,282,718 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Sudan ranks #29 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.642% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.03% a year (+1,620,571 people). The median age is 19.8 years, women have 4.13 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.9 years. Population density is 29 people per km² of land. The capital is Khartoum.
