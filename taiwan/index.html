    Taiwan population (2026)

    The population of Taiwan is 23,011,292 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Taiwan ranks #61 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.277% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.44% a year (-101,501 people). The median age is 46.4 years, women have 0.86 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.1 years. Population density is 636 people per km² of land. The capital is Taipei.
