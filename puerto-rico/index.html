    Puerto Rico population (2026)

    The population of Puerto Rico is 3,222,688 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Puerto Rico ranks #135 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.039% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.47% a year (-12,602 people). The median age is 47.2 years, women have 0.94 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.3 years. Population density is 363 people per km² of land. The capital is San Juan.
