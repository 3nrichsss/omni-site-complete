    Kosovo population (2026)

    The population of Kosovo is 1,666,992 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kosovo ranks #154 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.020% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.31% a year (-7,133 people). The median age is 34.2 years, women have 1.52 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.6 years. Population density is 153 people per km² of land. The capital is Pristina.
