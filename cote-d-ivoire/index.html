    Côte d'Ivoire population (2026)

    The population of Côte d'Ivoire is 33,494,346 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Côte d'Ivoire ranks #50 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.404% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.34% a year (+782,799 people). The median age is 19.5 years, women have 4.1 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 62.4 years. Population density is 105 people per km² of land. The capital is Yamoussoukro.
