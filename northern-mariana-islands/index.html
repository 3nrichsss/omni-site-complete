    Northern Mariana Islands population (2026)

    The population of Northern Mariana Islands is 42,914 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Northern Mariana Islands ranks #215 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 1.30% a year (-626 people). The median age is 39.8 years, women have 2.24 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.2 years. Population density is 94 people per km² of land. The capital is Saipan.
