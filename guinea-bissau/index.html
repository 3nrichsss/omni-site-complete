    Guinea-Bissau population (2026)

    The population of Guinea-Bissau is 2,297,808 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guinea-Bissau ranks #148 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.028% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.10% a year (+48,293 people). The median age is 20.6 years, women have 3.62 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.6 years. Population density is 82 people per km² of land. The capital is Bissau.
