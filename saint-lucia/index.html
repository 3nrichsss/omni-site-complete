    Saint Lucia population (2026)

    The population of Saint Lucia is 180,488 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Lucia ranks #191 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.17% a year (+339 people). The median age is 36.1 years, women have 1.37 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.2 years. Population density is 296 people per km² of land. The capital is Castries.
