    Slovakia population (2026)

    The population of Slovakia is 5,451,342 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Slovakia ranks #124 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.066% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.16% a year (-23,539 people). The median age is 43.8 years, women have 1.57 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.8 years. Population density is 113 people per km² of land. The capital is Bratislava.
