    Montserrat population (2026)

    The population of Montserrat is 4,328 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Montserrat ranks #233 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.83% a year (-31 people). The median age is 42.8 years, women have 1.45 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.7 years. Population density is 42 people per km² of land. The capital is Plymouth.
