    Serbia population (2026)

    The population of Serbia is 6,641,964 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Serbia ranks #111 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.080% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.66% a year (-47,075 people). The median age is 45.7 years, women have 1.51 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.3 years. Population density is 76 people per km² of land. The capital is Belgrade.
