    Chad population (2026)

    The population of Chad is 21,560,380 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Chad ranks #64 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.260% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.60% a year (+556,675 people). The median age is 16.7 years, women have 5.84 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 55.6 years. Population density is 17 people per km² of land. The capital is N'Djamena.
