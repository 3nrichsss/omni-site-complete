    Kenya population (2026)

    The population of Kenya is 58,636,412 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kenya ranks #26 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.706% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.89% a year (+1,103,919 people). The median age is 21.3 years, women have 3.07 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 64.2 years. Population density is 101 people per km² of land. The capital is Nairobi.
