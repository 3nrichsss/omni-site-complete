    United Kingdom population (2026)

    The population of United Kingdom is 69,931,528 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. United Kingdom ranks #22 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.842% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.52% a year (+380,197 people). The median age is 41.2 years, women have 1.53 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.7 years. Population density is 289 people per km² of land. The capital is London.
