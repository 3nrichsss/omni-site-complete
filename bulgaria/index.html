    Bulgaria population (2026)

    The population of Bulgaria is 6,667,660 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Bulgaria ranks #110 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.080% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.73% a year (-46,900 people). The median age is 46.0 years, women have 1.74 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.1 years. Population density is 61 people per km² of land. The capital is Sofia.
