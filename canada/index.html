    Canada population (2026)

    The population of Canada is 40,467,728 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Canada ranks #38 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.488% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.79% a year (+341,005 people). The median age is 41.8 years, women have 1.33 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.1 years. Population density is 5 people per km² of land. The capital is Ottawa.
