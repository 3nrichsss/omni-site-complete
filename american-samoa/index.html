    American Samoa population (2026)

    The population of American Samoa is 45,318 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. American Samoa ranks #213 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 1.54% a year (-710 people). The median age is 30.1 years, women have 2.22 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 73.3 years. Population density is 228 people per km² of land. The capital is Pago Pago.
