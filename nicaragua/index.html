    Nicaragua population (2026)

    The population of Nicaragua is 7,097,329 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Nicaragua ranks #108 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.086% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.25% a year (+89,827 people). The median age is 27.3 years, women have 2.16 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.4 years. Population density is 59 people per km² of land. The capital is Managua.
