    Zambia population (2026)

    The population of Zambia is 22,521,916 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Zambia ranks #63 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.271% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.72% a year (+608,041 people). The median age is 19.2 years, women have 3.91 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 66.9 years. Population density is 30 people per km² of land. The capital is Lusaka.
