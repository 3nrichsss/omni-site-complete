    Wallis and Futuna population (2026)

    The population of Wallis and Futuna is 11,113 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Wallis and Futuna ranks #229 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.72% a year (-81 people). The median age is 39.6 years, women have 1.39 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.0 years. Population density is 78 people per km² of land. The capital is Mata-Utu.
