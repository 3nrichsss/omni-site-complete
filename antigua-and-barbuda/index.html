    Antigua and Barbuda population (2026)

    The population of Antigua and Barbuda is 94,626 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Antigua and Barbuda ranks #201 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.43% a year (+417 people). The median age is 37.7 years, women have 1.57 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.1 years. Population density is 215 people per km² of land. The capital is Saint John’s.
