    Isle of Man population (2026)

    The population of Isle of Man is 84,054 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Isle of Man ranks #202 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.08% a year (-64 people). The median age is 47.3 years, women have 1.53 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.4 years. Population density is 147 people per km² of land. The capital is Douglas.
