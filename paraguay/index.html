    Paraguay population (2026)

    The population of Paraguay is 7,095,280 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Paraguay ranks #109 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.085% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.15% a year (+82,202 people). The median age is 28.3 years, women have 2.37 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.2 years. Population density is 18 people per km² of land. The capital is Asunción.
