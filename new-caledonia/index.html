    New Caledonia population (2026)

    The population of New Caledonia is 297,892 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. New Caledonia ranks #185 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.83% a year (+2,559 people). The median age is 35.8 years, women have 1.93 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.2 years. Population density is 16 people per km² of land. The capital is Nouméa.
