    Spain population (2026)

    The population of Spain is 47,850,792 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Spain ranks #34 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.576% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.10% a year (-39,165 people). The median age is 47.3 years, women have 1.24 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.1 years. Population density is 96 people per km² of land. The capital is Madrid.
