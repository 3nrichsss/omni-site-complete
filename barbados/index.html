    Barbados population (2026)

    The population of Barbados is 282,724 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Barbados ranks #187 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.03% a year (+101 people). The median age is 40.7 years, women have 1.7 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.6 years. Population density is 656 people per km² of land. The capital is Bridgetown.
