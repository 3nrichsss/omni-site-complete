    Oman population (2026)

    The population of Oman is 5,671,458 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Oman ranks #120 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.068% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.82% a year (+176,767 people). The median age is 30.8 years, women have 2.44 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.7 years. Population density is 18 people per km² of land. The capital is Muscat.
