    Malawi population (2026)

    The population of Malawi is 22,785,535 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Malawi ranks #62 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.275% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.53% a year (+569,415 people). The median age is 19.4 years, women have 3.45 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.9 years. Population density is 242 people per km² of land. The capital is Lilongwe.
