    Guam population (2026)

    The population of Guam is 170,185 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guam ranks #192 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.69% a year (+1,186 people). The median age is 32.6 years, women have 2.68 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.8 years. Population density is 315 people per km² of land. The capital is Hagåtña.
