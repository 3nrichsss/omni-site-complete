    Egypt population (2026)

    The population of Egypt is 120,101,176 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Egypt ranks #13 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.45% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.44% a year (+1,735,181 people). The median age is 25.7 years, women have 2.68 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.2 years. Population density is 121 people per km² of land. The capital is Cairo.
