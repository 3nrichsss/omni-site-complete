    Cape Verde population (2026)

    The population of Cape Verde is 529,630 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cape Verde ranks #175 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.42% a year (+2,304 people). The median age is 30.5 years, women have 1.49 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.6 years. Population density is 131 people per km² of land. The capital is Praia.
