    Monaco population (2026)

    The population of Monaco is 38,087 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Monaco ranks #219 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.58% a year (-254 people). The median age is 54.1 years, women have 2.08 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 86.7 years. Population density is 18,276 people per km² of land. The capital is Monaco.
