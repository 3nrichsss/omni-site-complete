    DR Congo population (2026)

    The population of DR Congo is 116,452,162 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. DR Congo ranks #15 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.40% of the world population. The population is growing by 3.13% a year (+3,619,689 people). The median age is 16.9 years, women have 5.8 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 62.4 years. Population density is 51 people per km² of land. The capital is Kinshasa.
