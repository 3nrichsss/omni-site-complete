    Malaysia population (2026)

    The population of Malaysia is 36,385,114 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Malaysia ranks #45 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.438% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.10% a year (+407,276 people). The median age is 32.4 years, women have 1.52 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.2 years. Population density is 111 people per km² of land. The capital is Kuala Lumpur.
