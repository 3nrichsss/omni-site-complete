    Cook Islands population (2026)

    The population of Cook Islands is 12,826 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cook Islands ranks #226 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 3.26% a year (-437 people). The median age is 39.1 years, women have 1.97 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.8 years. Population density is 54 people per km² of land. The capital is Avarua.
