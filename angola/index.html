    Angola population (2026)

    The population of Angola is 40,215,180 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Angola ranks #39 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.484% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.95% a year (+1,175,141 people). The median age is 17.7 years, women have 4.89 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 65.2 years. Population density is 32 people per km² of land. The capital is Luanda.
