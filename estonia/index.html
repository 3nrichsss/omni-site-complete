    Estonia population (2026)

    The population of Estonia is 1,331,062 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Estonia ranks #158 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.016% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.53% a year (-13,170 people). The median age is 44.2 years, women have 1.38 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.6 years. Population density is 31 people per km² of land. The capital is Tallinn.
