    Belize population (2026)

    The population of Belize is 428,644 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Belize ranks #177 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.33% a year (+5,721 people). The median age is 28.3 years, women have 1.99 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 74.1 years. Population density is 19 people per km² of land. The capital is Belmopan.
