    Peru population (2026)

    The population of Peru is 34,922,148 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Peru ranks #48 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.421% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.97% a year (+345,483 people). The median age is 31.5 years, women have 1.92 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.3 years. Population density is 27 people per km² of land. The capital is Lima.
