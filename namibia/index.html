    Namibia population (2026)

    The population of Namibia is 3,153,246 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Namibia ranks #137 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.038% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.89% a year (+60,430 people). The median age is 22.5 years, women have 3.13 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 67.8 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Windhoek.
