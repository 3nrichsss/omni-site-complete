    Saudi Arabia population (2026)

    The population of Saudi Arabia is 35,165,787 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saudi Arabia ranks #47 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.424% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.69% a year (+599,459 people). The median age is 30.7 years, women have 2.27 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 79.4 years. Population density is 16 people per km² of land. The capital is Riyadh.
