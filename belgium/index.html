    Belgium population (2026)

    The population of Belgium is 11,774,642 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Belgium ranks #83 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.142% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.12% a year (+16,039 people). The median age is 43.1 years, women have 1.4 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.6 years. Population density is 386 people per km² of land. The capital is Brussels.
