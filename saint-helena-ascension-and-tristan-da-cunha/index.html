    Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha population (2026)

    The population of Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha is 5,148 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha ranks #232 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 1.15% a year (-49 people).
