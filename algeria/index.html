    Algeria population (2026)

    The population of Algeria is 48,028,334 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Algeria ranks #32 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.579% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.20% a year (+593,022 people). The median age is 29.8 years, women have 2.62 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.9 years. Population density is 20 people per km² of land. The capital is Algiers.
