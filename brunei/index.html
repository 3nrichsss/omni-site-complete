    Brunei population (2026)

    The population of Brunei is 469,775 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Brunei ranks #176 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.72% a year (+3,444 people). The median age is 34.2 years, women have 1.7 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.8 years. Population density is 89 people per km² of land. The capital is Bandar Seri Begawan.
