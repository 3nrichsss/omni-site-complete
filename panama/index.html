    Panama population (2026)

    The population of Panama is 4,625,718 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Panama ranks #129 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.056% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.17% a year (+54,529 people). The median age is 31.6 years, women have 2.07 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.1 years. Population density is 62 people per km² of land. The capital is Panamá.
