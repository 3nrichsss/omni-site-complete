    Palau population (2026)

    The population of Palau is 17,614 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Palau ranks #224 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.30% a year (-48 people). The median age is 39.9 years, women have 1.84 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 69.6 years. Population density is 38 people per km² of land. The capital is Ngerulmud.
