    Luxembourg population (2026)

    The population of Luxembourg is 687,448 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Luxembourg ranks #169 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.99% a year (+6,995 people). The median age is 40.7 years, women have 1.41 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.6 years. Population density is 267 people per km² of land. The capital is Luxembourg.
