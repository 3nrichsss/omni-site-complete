    Vietnam population (2026)

    The population of Vietnam is 102,177,431 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Vietnam ranks #16 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.23% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.55% a year (+578,904 people). The median age is 34.9 years, women have 1.86 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 75.0 years. Population density is 326 people per km² of land. The capital is Hanoi.
