    Saint Vincent and the Grenadines population (2026)

    The population of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines is 99,244 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Saint Vincent and the Grenadines ranks #200 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.68% a year (-680 people). The median age is 35.7 years, women have 1.73 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 71.7 years. Population density is 255 people per km² of land. The capital is Kingstown.
