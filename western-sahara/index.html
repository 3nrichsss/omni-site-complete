    Western Sahara population (2026)

    The population of Western Sahara is 610,813 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Western Sahara ranks #172 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.58% a year (+9,909 people). The median age is 33.8 years, women have 2.13 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 72.0 years. Population density is 2 people per km² of land. The capital is Laayoune.
