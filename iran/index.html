    Iran population (2026)

    The population of Iran is 93,168,498 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Iran ranks #17 of 236 countries and territories and holds 1.12% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.76% a year (+750,817 people). The median age is 35.5 years, women have 1.66 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 78.2 years. Population density is 57 people per km² of land. The capital is Tehran.
