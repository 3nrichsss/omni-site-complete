    Guinea population (2026)

    The population of Guinea is 15,441,992 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Guinea ranks #75 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.186% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.21% a year (+342,265 people). The median age is 19.5 years, women have 3.96 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 61.2 years. Population density is 63 people per km² of land. The capital is Conakry.
