    Andorra population (2026)

    The population of Andorra is 83,753 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Andorra ranks #203 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.96% a year (+849 people). The median age is 45.2 years, women have 1.11 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.5 years. Population density is 179 people per km² of land. The capital is Andorra la Vella.
