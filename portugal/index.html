    Portugal population (2026)

    The population of Portugal is 10,395,362 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Portugal ranks #94 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.125% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.16% a year (-16,471 people). The median age is 48.2 years, women have 1.52 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.9 years. Population density is 113 people per km² of land. The capital is Lisbon.
