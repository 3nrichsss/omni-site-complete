    Aruba population (2026)

    The population of Aruba is 108,164 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Aruba ranks #197 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.02% a year (+16 people). The median age is 42.7 years, women have 1.61 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.8 years. Population density is 601 people per km² of land. The capital is Oranjestad.
