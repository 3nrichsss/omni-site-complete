    Qatar population (2026)

    The population of Qatar is 3,173,560 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Qatar ranks #136 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.038% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.65% a year (+57,671 people). The median age is 34.4 years, women have 1.69 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 82.8 years. Population density is 276 people per km² of land. The capital is Doha.
