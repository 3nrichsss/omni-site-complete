    Brazil population (2026)

    The population of Brazil is 213,562,666 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Brazil ranks #7 of 236 countries and territories and holds 2.57% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.34% a year (+750,261 people). The median age is 36.2 years, women have 1.59 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 76.4 years. Population density is 26 people per km² of land. The capital is Brasília.
