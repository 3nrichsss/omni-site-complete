    Kuwait population (2026)

    The population of Kuwait is 5,102,773 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Kuwait ranks #128 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.061% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.39% a year (+76,695 people). The median age is 35.8 years, women have 1.49 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 81.0 years. Population density is 286 people per km² of land. The capital is Kuwait City.
