    Rwanda population (2026)

    The population of Rwanda is 14,889,693 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Rwanda ranks #77 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.179% of the world population. The population is growing by 2.12% a year (+320,352 people). The median age is 21.2 years, women have 3.54 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 68.5 years. Population density is 627 people per km² of land. The capital is Kigali.
