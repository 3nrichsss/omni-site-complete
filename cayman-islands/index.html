    Cayman Islands population (2026)

    The population of Cayman Islands is 77,196 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Cayman Islands ranks #205 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 1.73% a year (+1,352 people). The median age is 40.1 years, women have 1.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 80.9 years. Population density is 322 people per km² of land. The capital is George Town.
