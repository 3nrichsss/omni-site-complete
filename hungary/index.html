    Hungary population (2026)

    The population of Hungary is 9,585,818 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Hungary ranks #98 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.115% of the world population. The population is shrinking by 0.34% a year (-46,469 people). The median age is 45.1 years, women have 1.5 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 77.5 years. Population density is 105 people per km² of land. The capital is Budapest.
