    Singapore population (2026)

    The population of Singapore is 5,905,748 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Singapore ranks #115 of 236 countries and territories and holds 0.071% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.56% a year (+34,998 people). The median age is 37.8 years, women have 0.97 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 84.1 years. Population density is 8,148 people per km² of land. The capital is Singapore.
