    Iceland population (2026)

    The population of Iceland is 402,329 as of 1 July 2026, according to UN World Population Prospects 2024. Iceland ranks #179 of 236 countries and territories and holds <0.01% of the world population. The population is growing by 0.92% a year (+4,063 people). The median age is 37.6 years, women have 1.49 children on average and life expectancy at birth is 83.3 years. Population density is 4 people per km² of land. The capital is Reykjavík.
